5 Landing Page Fixes That Lower Cost Per Lead

Before you raise your ad budget, fix the page the traffic lands on. These five changes make the biggest difference.

September 10, 2026Conversion5 min readPPC Birds

Many campaigns that look like ad problems are actually landing page problems. The ad does its job, then the visitor lands somewhere slow, generic or confusing.

1. Match the message to the ad

If the ad promises emergency plumbing, the page headline should say emergency plumbing, not “Welcome to our company.”

2. Load fast on mobile

Most paid clicks come from phones. Compress images, remove unnecessary scripts and test on a real mobile connection.

3. One page, one action

Remove navigation menus and competing links. Give visitors one clear thing to do.

4. Show proof early

  • Reviews and star ratings
  • Recognizable client logos
  • Specific results with numbers

5. Ask for less

Every extra form field costs conversions. Ask only for what you need to follow up, and collect the rest later.

“A better landing page lowers your cost per lead on every channel at once.”PPC Birds CRO team
