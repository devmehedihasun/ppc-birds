Overview

Built Around the Numbers That Matter

Microsoft Advertising serves ads on Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo and across the Microsoft network. Its audience skews older and higher-income, and competition is lighter, which often means lower costs per click than Google.

Because Google campaigns can be imported, Microsoft Ads is usually the quickest second channel to launch. We import, then adjust bids, audiences and ad copy so the campaigns perform on their own terms.
